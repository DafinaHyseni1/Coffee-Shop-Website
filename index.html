ABOUT US

The origins of our coffee shop are rooted in a deep appreciation for the art of coffee-making. From the very beginning, we sought to offer an experience that goes beyond the usual cup of coffee.

Every bean is chosen not just for its quality, but for its unique story – from the farms of Ethiopia, where coffee is believed to have originated, to the lush mountains of Colombia, where the beans are nurtured to perfection.

THE COFFEE JOURNEY

01

Born in the Soil

Every bean starts its life in high-altitude farms where climate, soil, and care shape its identity long before it becomes coffee.

02

Harvested by Hand

Only the ripest cherries are handpicked with precision — no machines, just human experience and patience.

03

Roasted with Precision

We roast in micro-batches to unlock deep aroma layers, balancing boldness, acidity, and natural sweetness.

04

Served as an Experience

What you drink is not just coffee — it’s a crafted moment designed to slow time and elevate your senses.
